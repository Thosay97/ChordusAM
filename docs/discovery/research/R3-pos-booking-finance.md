# R3: POS, studio booking and rental, SME finance, and Sri Lanka payments and tax

**Date:** 9 November 2026
**Author:** R3, Market Researcher (discovery team)
**Upstream read:** `docs/discovery/00-brief.md`, `docs/PLAN.md` sections 5, 6 and 9.

**Summary.** AMPL sells studio time, photo sessions, prints and merch, equipment rental and production services. This report surveys three groups of tools: point-of-sale (POS) systems, studio, room and equipment booking and rental tools, and SME accounting packages. It also covers the Sri Lankan payment rails and the tax rules that shape a POS or invoice in 2026. The main findings are these. First, no tool surveyed covers AMPL's mix of service bookings, deposits, equipment check-out and Sri Lankan tax in one place. Retail POS tools are weak on services and deposits. Studio CRMs are strong on contracts and payment schedules, but they are US-centric and rely on Stripe, Square or PayPal. Rental tools like Rentman are strong on equipment, but they are priced per user in EUR or USD. Second, Sri Lankan tax is changing fast in 2026. A new VAT tax invoice format became mandatory on 1 October 2026. The SSCL registration threshold dropped to LKR 36m a year on 1 July 2026. Withholding tax now covers artists, performers and photographers. A national e-invoicing system linked to RAMIS (the IRD's tax system) is rolling out to VAT-registered businesses. Third, the sensible design is to **build** a service-first POS, booking and rental layer inside ChordusAM, and to **integrate** for the books of record (Xero, QuickBooks Online or Zoho Books, whichever the accountant uses) and for online payments (PayHere first). Every tax rate and threshold must be data, not code.

### 2.2 What this means for AMPL

1. **Retail POS tools do not handle a studio's money flow.** AMPL's flow is: booking, then deposit, then balance on the day, then add-ons, then a link to a project. Only Square (Appointments) models deposits natively, and Square cannot take cards in Sri Lanka. Shopify, Lightspeed, Loyverse and the local tools are built around retail baskets.
2. **Offline mode is table stakes, but with limits everywhere.** Every vendor restricts what works offline: gift cards, new customers, discounts and refunds. The common pattern is a queue of sales on the device that syncs later, with cash and pre-approved methods only. ChordusAM can copy this. Cash and a "card paid on bank terminal, approval code X" entry can be queued offline. Anything that changes shared state (deposit balances, vouchers) is blocked until the device reconnects.
3. **Card payments in a shop in Sri Lanka go through a standalone bank terminal**, not through the POS software (§5). So POS software here mostly records the method and reference. That makes building a light POS cheaper. ChordusAM does not need payment-terminal certification.
4. **Sri Lanka will link B2C POS to the IRD later.** The plan is to link retail POS machines to the IRD for real-time sales reporting, after the B2B phases ([vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/sri-lanka/sri-lanka-e-invoicing-plans/), [PCWorld LK](https://pcworld.lk/magazine/?p=146)) (secondary). Any POS AMPL adopts must be able to send invoice data to RAMIS later. That favours owning the data model.

### 3.4 What this means for AMPL

1. **The best UX patterns to copy:**
   - Pixieset's **quote with several packages and add-ons, then contract, then deposit and payment schedule** flow, for photo sessions and production quotes.
   - Skedda's **rules engine**: price by space, time and user group, plus buffers, approvals and no-show release. Use it for studio rooms.
   - Rentman's **scan-to-pack and scan-to-return, with repairs and a "charge damage on invoice" option**, for gear.
   - Odoo Rental's **late fee per extra hour or day, padding time and security time**, as configurable policy data. This fits the CLAUDE.md rule "company policy is data edited in the UI".
2. **Most Western booking and CRM tools depend on Stripe, Square or PayPal for deposits.** Square does not process cards in Sri Lanka (§2.1). Whether Stripe supports Sri Lankan merchants was **not checked** in this session because the search budget ran out (unverified). My understanding is that it does not, so confirm this before any buy decision. HoneyBook explicitly excludes Sri Lanka. So these tools would either collect no deposits, or need a foreign entity. AMPL may have one later through the SG entity, but the MVP should not depend on it.
3. **Conflict detection is less solid than it looks.** Skedda blocks overlaps. Odoo's padding time was reported to let overlaps through. ChordusAM should enforce "no double booking" in the database (a Postgres exclusion constraint on resource and time range), not only in the UI. This matches the CLAUDE.md rule on server-side enforcement.
4. **AMPL's volume is small.** It has one studio, a modest kit list and about 10 to 30 staff. Rentman or Current RMS at about USD 19 to 62 per user per month is affordable for 2 or 3 power users, but it would be a second system holding projects and crew. Recommendation: build a minimal asset and rental module in ChordusAM, using Rentman as the reference model. Revisit Rentman only if gear rental becomes a major revenue line.

### 4.2 What this means for AMPL

- PLAN §6 already says ChordusAM is a management ledger, not statutory accounting: "keep the accountant's books ... give the accountant an export". The research supports keeping it that way. No package has strong Sri Lankan localisation. Bank feeds for Sri Lankan banks are unconfirmed in all of them. And the accountant's choice of package is outside AMPL's control.
- **Recommended step up from "export":** add a **sync adapter**. ChordusAM pushes approved invoices, receipts, vendor bills (paid payment requests) and payroll journals to the accountant's package through its API. Each record is tagged with project or tracking category and tax code, and an external ID is stored for each pushed record. Xero (Colombo partner base, clear limits) or QuickBooks Online are the likely first targets. **Ask Janaka which package the accountant uses** before choosing.
- **Multi-entity (SG):** every package above needs one subscription or company file per legal entity. ChordusAM should keep an `entity_id` on every money record and map each entity to its own accounting tenant.
