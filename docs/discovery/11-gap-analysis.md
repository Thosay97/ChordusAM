# ChordusAM gap analysis: today, the current plan and the market

**Date:** 9 November 2026 · **Author:** Business Analyst (discovery team) · **Upstream read:** `docs/discovery/00-brief.md`, `docs/discovery/10-brd.md` (BRD, the requirement IDs used here), `docs/PLAN.md` v2.0, `docs/phase-0.md`, the Phase 0 code (`lib/auth/`, `lib/audit.ts`, `lib/rules/`, `supabase/migrations/`, `app/`, `tests/`), research files R1 to R4, and `docs/discovery/12-benchmark-usp.md`, `13-ux-design.md`, `14-business-case.md` and `15-compliance-security.md`.

**Summary.** This file compares the BRD against three things. **Lens A** compares AMPL's current way of working (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, ClickUp and a fingerprint export) with the BRD's future state, process by process: 21 processes, 13 with high impact. The biggest gaps are money-related (freelancer WHT, invoicing and collection, month-end close, VAT and SSCL watch), creative-risk related (approvals, rights and consents) and legal (PDPA from 1 January 2027). **Lens B** compares the current ChordusAM plan (PLAN.md v2.0 and what Phase 0 actually built) with the BRD's 222 requirements. Phase 0 built a sound security foundation but no business function: 1 requirement is built and 2 are partly built in Phase 0, 66 are planned in a PLAN.md phase, 28 are partly planned, and 125 (56%) are not planned at all. All of POS, BKG, CRM (except the client list), TAL, AST and most of AI and SAL are new. **Lens C** compares nine market products with ChordusAM. No comparator covers freelancer WHT, rights and consent records, or AI cost per video, which is where building wins. Buying wins for the video player, the books of record, payment processing, card terminals and possibly statutory payroll. The file ends with the 15 gap closures to do first.

## How to read this file

- Requirement IDs (`BR-…`, `NFR-…`) are defined in `docs/discovery/10-brd.md`. Open questions (`OQ-…`) are in BRD section 13.3.
- **Impact** in lens A: **H** = money lost, legal exposure or a blocked business line; **M** = real time or quality cost; **L** = inconvenience.
- **Status** in lens B, per requirement:
  - **Built (Phase 0):** working code and tests exist today.
  - **Partly built (Phase 0):** Phase 0 provides part of it (for example the table, guard or trigger) but not the user-facing function.
  - **Planned (PLAN Pn):** PLAN.md v2.0 specifies it fully and assigns it to phase n.
  - **Partly planned (PLAN Pn):** PLAN.md mentions it or covers part of it.
  - **Not planned:** not in PLAN.md.
- Lens C symbols are explained above the heatmap.
