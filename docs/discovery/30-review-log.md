# 30: Cross-document review log for the ChordusAM discovery pack

**Date:** 10 November 2026 · **Author:** Reviewer (discovery team) · **Status:** Review done; edits applied to the pack, nothing committed

**Summary.** I reviewed the twelve discovery documents (10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 20, 21, 22, 22a, 23, 24) against each other, against the research where a claim needed checking, and against the repository (`package.json`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `supabase/config.toml`). I found **18 issues: 3 high, 8 medium and 7 low**. The three high ones were real contradictions an owner would act on: the BRD still showed the BA's 182-requirement MVP after the Product Owner cut it to 133; the solution design's RLS pattern was the one the spike measured as 50 times slower; and the pack carried two incompatible estimates (PO: 14 weeks, about LKR 5.4m; Tech Lead: about 274 ideal engineer-days, MVP on 2 July 2027, about LKR 12m). I fixed the first two in place with small, marked edits. For the third I did not pick a number: the documents now point to `22-delivery-plan.md` as the current best estimate, and the funding option is recorded as an owner decision. A scripted check found **no broken requirement cross-references** (BR, NFR, BRULE, OQ, EP, SPK, CFO and G IDs). The pack is good enough to start Release 0 once the owner takes the four decisions due on 20 November 2026 (section 4).

- Read `00-brief.md` and `README.md`, then the summary, decision and estimate sections of every document. I read specific sections rather than whole files, and used the research files only to check claims.

Result: 0 undefined IDs, and no BR or NFR IDs in the wrong format.
- **MVP mapping, scripted.** The release lists in `21-mvp-roadmap.md` §6.1 were parsed (R0 13, MVP 120, R1 48, R1\* 8, R2 30, Won't 3 = 222, each ID exactly once) and written into the BRD's "MVP?" column. The per-module totals match 21's table exactly.
- **Repository facts** were checked directly. I did not run any web searches, so claims about vendor support windows are marked "(verify)" rather than confirmed or removed.
